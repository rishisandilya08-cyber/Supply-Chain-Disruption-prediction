📖 Plain-English Guide to Sensor Words

Health Score (0 - 100%) Like machine battery life. 100% is fresh out of the box. Below 50% means wear is severe and a fix should happen soon to avoid sudden stoppage.
Shaking (Vibration) How much the machine rattles. A smooth machine hums gently (< 1.5 mm/s). Heavy shaking (> 4 mm/s) means loose parts or bad bearings.
Safe Hours Left (RUL) How many hours the machine can keep making good products before it could break down. Gives our team time to fix it during shift breaks.
Fix Plan (Prescriptive) Exact instructions for the mechanic: which spare part to grab, what tools to take, and when to fix it without stopping production line quota.
