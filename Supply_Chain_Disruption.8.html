Supply Chain Disruption Prediction

Problem

Production delays happen because supplier, inventory and transport problems are spotted too late. Detect emerging risks early and recommend mitigation.

Algorithm flow

InputProcessDecisionOutput
Stream supplier OTIF, lead-time, inventory, production plan, shipment tracking
Compute leading indicators: lead-time drift, OTIF decline, days-of-cover, transit delay
Normalise each indicator 0–1
Weighted risk score per supplier / part (weights adjustable)
Risk ≥ threshold or days-of-cover < lead time ?
YES → raise alert; estimate production impact (days at risk)
Pick mitigation: expedite, alternate supplier, buffer stock, re-route
Ranked risk board + mitigation actions

Pseudocode

for s in suppliers:
    s.drift  = (s.lead_time_30d - s.lead_time_90d) / s.lead_time_90d
    s.otif_d = s.otif_90d - s.otif_30d
    s.cover  = s.stock / s.daily_use
    s.risk   = w1*norm(drift) + w2*norm(otif_d) + w3*norm(1/cover) + w4*norm(transit_delay)
    if s.risk >= T or s.cover < s.lead_time:
        alert(s, days_at_risk = s.lead_time - s.cover)
        s.action = choose(MITIGATIONS, s.dominant_driver)
